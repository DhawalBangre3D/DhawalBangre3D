About

I'm Dhawal, a 3D artist specializing in automotive and product visualization. My work centers on lighting and rendering prepped CAD data to production quality, using a Maya and V-Ray pipeline professionally, supported by 3ds Max, Substance Painter, and Unreal Engine 5 in my own practice.

Based in Pune, India.

Alongside client work, I maintain a set of personal technical projects — including working on my motorcycle and DIY audio electronics — that reflect the same attention to material behavior and precision I bring to professional renders. I have also begun integrating generative AI tools into my workflow to improve production speed without compromising output quality.

My portfolio spans vehicle and product visualization, real-time work built in Unreal Engine 5, and environment reconstruction.
